Christopher GWS CLI

Christopher GWS CLI is a private command-line tool used by its owner to work with his own Google Workspace account (such as Gmail, Google Drive, Google Calendar and Google Sheets) from the terminal.

What the app does

  • Signs in with Google OAuth so the owner can access his own Workspace data.
  • Reads, searches and organizes emails, files, calendar events and spreadsheets on request.
  • Runs entirely on the owner's own computer. It is not a public service and has no user accounts of its own.

Privacy

Data accessed through Google APIs stays on the owner's computer and is never sold or shared with third parties. Read the full Privacy Policy.
